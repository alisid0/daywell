# Daywell promo "Small things count": script for approval

24 October 2026. 2 minutes 30 seconds, vertical 1080 × 1920, for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It's built from **30 moments of 5 seconds each**, as asked, then compiled into one film. Each moment can also be cut out and posted on its own. It shows the app as it is today, main `46eab3e`, deployed to the private test app on 24 October, with the sticker look, the new time wheels and the week strip.

Nothing has been made yet: no voice, no music and no film. Everything waits for your approval of this script.

## The idea

**Claim (said in the first 5 seconds):** small things count.

**One line:** looking after yourself feels like a lot, so Daywell breaks it into small things. Note a night, snap a meal, take a short walk, have a quiet minute. Each one counts, and none of them asks for more than a moment.

**Signature move: the sticker.** Every 5-second moment ends with a sticker stamp landing beside the phone: Noted, Saved, Planned, Counts, Rested. Stamps are what the app's new sticker look is made of, and they feel like a small win without being a score. The stamp from one moment shrinks into the button that starts the next, so the film flows instead of cutting. At the end, all the stickers gather into a page, the page folds into the Daywell sun, and the end card appears. There are no numbers, counters or progress bars, because Daywell has no streaks or scores.

**DECISION:** "Small things count" is the claim. It's close to Bounce's real line, "A stretch counts. A small walk counts, too.", and to the app's "rest days count too". It doesn't claim that anything takes exactly 5 seconds.

## Sound

- **Music:** a new ElevenLabs track, instrumental, composed for exactly 150 seconds at **96 BPM**, so every 5-second moment is exactly two bars and the cuts land on the downbeat. The real beats are measured from the finished track and the film is moved to fit them.

  | Section | Moments | Time | Feel |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Busy | 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Quick plucks, a little hectic |
  | Reveal | 2 | 0:05–0:10 | A warm open chord on "Daywell" |
  | Morning | 3–12 | 0:10–1:00 | Light, bright groove |
  | Move | 13–18 | 1:00–1:30 | The most energy |
  | Afternoon | 19–21 | 1:30–1:45 | Easing back |
  | Dusk | 22–24 | 1:45–2:00 | Drums out, soft keys |
  | Night | 25–26 | 2:00–2:10 | Very sparse, room for the real "Waves on sand" |
  | Dawn and end | 27–30 | 2:10–2:30 | The groove returns, then a warm final chord |

- **Sound effects:** the 15 made for the October film are reused (tap, shutter, saved chime, pop, typing, timer, swoosh, scroll, shimmer and others). Two new ones: a soft **sticker slap** for each stamp and a quiet **wheel click** for the spinning wheels.
- **Mix and master:** the voice on top, with the music ducked under it, then mastered to −14 LUFS with true peak at or below −1 dBTP, as before.

## Look

- Daywell's default look: the pink speckle ground, the sticker personality (ink outlines and hard shadows), and forest, peach and mint.
- **One accent, forest `#2f7a55`**, used only for the finger's tap ripple and the stamps. Everything else is ground, ink and muted colours.
- Words in Bricolage Grotesque, two lines at most, inside the safe area: nothing important in the bottom 20%, the right 12% or the top 10%.
- The real app screens are captured from today's main in the test copy of the app, with sample data and the story's own clock. Only the moving parts are rebuilt on top, in the app's own styles.
- The sky goes from dawn through day and dusk to night, then dawn again for the last four moments.

## Never

- Never call Daywell a dating app. "A date with yourself" is self-care wordplay on daywell.date.
- Everyday wellness only: no claims about better sleep, weight, anxiety or health. "Drift off to waves on sand" describes the sound, not a promise about sleep.
- No free trial, because there isn't one.
- No invented numbers, ratings, user counts or reviews, and no streaks, scores or progress bars, including the stickers.
- No stock faces and no other company's logos.
- Nothing that only works with the app open or isn't built: no alarms, no reminders and notifications, and no live voice. The assistant is shown typed.
- Post only when everything shown works on real phones in the store apps, including subscriptions at these prices.
